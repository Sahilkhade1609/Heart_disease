Understanding Coronary Artery Disease (CAD)

What is CAD?

CAD occurs when coronary arteries narrow due to plaque buildup (atherosclerosis), reducing blood supply to the heart muscle.

How It Happens

High cholesterol, smoking, diabetes, and hypertension damage arteries, leading to progressive blockage.

Why Dangerous?

Reduced blood flow may cause angina, heart attack, heart failure, or sudden cardiac death.

Prevention

Healthy diet, exercise, BP control, no smoking, stress management, and early screening.
