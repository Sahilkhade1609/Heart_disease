Understanding Coronary Artery Disease (CAD)

What is CAD?

CAD occurs when coronary arteries narrow due to plaque buildup (atherosclerosis), reducing blood supply to the heart muscle.

How It Happens

High cholesterol, smoking, diabetes, and hypertension damage arteries, leading to progressive blockage.

Why Dangerous?

Reduced blood flow may cause angina, heart attack, heart failure, or sudden cardiac death.

Prevention

Healthy diet, exercise, BP control, no smoking, stress management, and early screening.

Coronary Artery Disease Stages

1. Healthy Artery Blood flows freely through the artery, supplying the heart muscle with enough oxygen and nutrients.

2. Initial Fatty Deposits Small amounts of cholesterol, fat, and other substances begin to accumulate on the inner walls of the artery.

3. Plaque Obstruction Over time these deposits form plaque which narrows the artery and reduces blood flow.

4. Near Complete Blockage Severe plaque buildup may completely block the artery and can cause a heart attack.
